GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before your first contract.

?
Can I install AutoContract now?+

The app is in Closed Testing. You need a Google account authorised for testing and must join the programme. Use the same account for enrolment and installation. Contact the developer if you do not have access.

What can I do for free?+

Read documents with OCR, enter details manually and preview Romanian and international contracts. The free preview carries a red watermark. Saving, sharing and printing the final document without a watermark requires Personal or Pro.

Does it work without the internet?+

OCR and PDF generation run locally. Internet access is needed for purchases, restoring and verifying Google Play licences, and Google Drive features.

Do I need to check automatically read details?+

Yes. OCR can make mistakes. Compare details with the originals, correct suggestions and manually confirm personal identification numbers and the VIN. The app does not replace checks of the documents or transaction conditions.

Can I import the front and back of an identity card together?+

Yes. For cards with information on both sides, you can select the two photos together from your phone during import. The extracted details still need to be checked.

Can I charge for preparing contracts with Personal?+

Personal is licensed for non-commercial use. Commercial activity, including paid contract preparation services, requires a valid Pro subscription.

Do I need Personal before subscribing to Pro?+

No. Pro provides its features independently. If you also purchased Personal, that licence remains active after Pro expires, with the rights and limitations of the Personal plan.

Is my archive deleted when Pro expires?+

No. Expiry does not delete the existing archive. Access returns to the rights provided by your valid licences, while recovery and deletion of existing data remain available.

Are international contracts official forms?+

They are private templates adapted to the selected country, completed manually. They are not presented as certified official forms and acceptance by authorities is not guaranteed. Check the requirements for your transaction and the country where you will use the document.
