4. Trial / Free

Installation, OCR, entry, review and correction of data, and PDF preview are free. Protected previews display the red Romanian watermark SPECIMEN – FĂRĂ VALOARE JURIDICĂ (SPECIMEN – NO LEGAL VALUE), and Android may block screen capture on that screen. Saving, printing and sharing the clean final contract require a valid Personal or Pro entitlement.

5. AutoContract Personal

Personal is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. The reference price for Romania at the revision date is RON 99.99. While the purchase is valid, the licence allows unlimited generation of Romanian and international agreements and export of clean PDFs for personal, non-commercial use. It does not include the internal archive, client and vehicle records, or Drive sync for new contracts. You can explicitly save a PDF to your chosen destination. Paid contract-preparation services for third parties require the Pro commercial licence.

6. AutoContract Pro / Professional

Pro is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. The reference prices for Romania at the revision date are RON 49.99 per month and RON 399.99 per year. While valid, it includes contract generation and export, the local archive, client and vehicle records and history, data reuse and optional Google Drive integration. It also permits commercial use of the application, including preparing documents for paying clients, within the limits of the law and your authority. Pro can be purchased directly without Personal.

Pro renews under the terms confirmed in Google Play until you stop renewal. Manage the subscription in the Google Play Subscriptions section. Uninstalling the application does not cancel it. A separate Personal purchase remains independent. Existing data and documents are not automatically deleted because of expiry and remain available through the applicable recovery and deletion options.
